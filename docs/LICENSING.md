contributors. It does not replace or alter the license of third-party material. In particular:

- the governed Deno and `rusty_v8` forks retain their upstream MIT terms;

- files under `artifacts/` that include their own `LICENSE`, source inventory, or notice set remain
  governed by those terms.

[`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) records notices for dependencies linked by the
current Go module. Runtime bundles and other distributable artifacts require their own complete,
version-specific license, notice, corresponding-source, and SBOM closure; the repository license
does not satisfy those obligations by itself.

inclusion in Capsule is licensed under Apache-2.0 as described by Section 5 of the license. Do not
submit copied or generated material unless its provenance and license permit inclusion and its
required notices are retained.

No contributor license agreement or copyright assignment is currently required. If Capsule later
needs proprietary dual licensing, that policy must be selected before accepting contributions
whose copyright would prevent that change.
